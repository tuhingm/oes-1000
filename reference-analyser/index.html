INTERPRETATION RULES

Evidence, not false certainty

A Crossref match confirms metadata correspondence, not that a source supports a manuscript's claim. A missing Crossref record does not prove a reference is fabricated: books, reports and records from other registries may be legitimate.

The analyser reports transparent diagnostics. It does not assign an opaque “authenticity score” or label a source fraudulent solely because metadata could not be retrieved.
